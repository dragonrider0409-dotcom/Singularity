What's inside

Any market
US & international stocks, crypto, and forex through Interactive Brokers.
Risk:reward on every trade
Bracket orders enforce your stop and target on each entry, automatically.
Adaptive learning
It records every trade and tilts position size toward conditions that have historically worked.
Live charts
Equity curve, per-trade P&L, and a panel showing what it has learned.
Medium frequency
Fast loop on short bars with momentum-breakout entries.
Paper by default
Starts on simulated money so you can test before risking a real dollar.

Before you trade

You'll need an Interactive Brokers account and their free IB Gateway running and logged in — the in-app checklist walks you through it (it's how any IB app reaches the market). Singularity runs on your computer, not in the cloud. And to be straight: no tool, this one included, can guarantee profit — keep it in Paper mode until the results hold up over many trades, and only risk money you can afford to lose.
